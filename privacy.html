Auspice — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Auspice ("the app") provides feng shui guidance for entertainment and self-reflection. We collect as little data as possible.

What the app uses

  • Birth date and sex — used on your device to compute your Kua number and personal directions. Not transmitted to us.
  • Compass heading / facing direction — read from your device sensors to orient your reading. Not stored or transmitted.
  • Room photos and scans (if you use scanning features) — processed to generate your reading. When AI analysis is enabled, photos are sent securely to our AI processing provider solely to analyze the room, are not used to train models, and are not retained beyond processing.
  • Purchases — handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no ads, no trackers, no sale or sharing of personal data.
  • No data is used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data deletion

Your data lives on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. For any question or request, contact us and we will respond promptly.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children.
